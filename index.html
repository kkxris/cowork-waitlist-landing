— the workshop

What to expect

Learn how to build and structure reusable .md skills for content creation and marketing use cases, so Claude Code generates consistent, high-quality outputs every time.

What to expect
  • Discover the framework we use to organize Notion as a knowledge base for Claude Code, eliminating the need for Notion AI while making the most of your Claude subscription.
  • Understand how to manage context, structure your knowledge, and reduce unnecessary token usage to build faster and more cost-effective workflows.
  • Create a content workflow that can generate ideas and content across multiple channels, including LinkedIn, Instagram, newsletters, blogs, Medium, and Substack.
  • Get the exact templates, .md files, and resources we use, sent before the workshop so you're ready to build from day one.
  • Hands-on guidance from mentors throughout the workshop for setup, debugging, and questions.
  • A deliberately small group, so you can ask questions, get feedback, and work through your own content and marketing use case.
  • Leave with a reusable AI-powered content system that takes you from research and ideation to content creation and reporting.
What you'll need
  • A free Notion account, the free plan is enough
  • A Claude plan, Pro or higher (Claude Code is included)
  • Your laptop and charger
  • No coding background needed, templates and .md files are sent before the session
By the end, you'll walk away with

The full campaign, end to end: Notion connected as your content database, ideas turned into a real plan, drafts for Instagram, LinkedIn, blog, and newsletter from one brief, everything moved between Claude Code and Notion, and a report on what went out and what's worth doing more of.

  • A working set of .md files that write in your voice
  • At least one skill you built and structured yourself
  • A real campaign, planned, drafted, and filed
  • A repeatable workflow instead of a scramble
Honest caveat: visual assets are where Claude stops. We'll tell you what to do instead, this workshop is about the writing and the system around it.

Who this is for

  • Founders who are also the marketing team
  • Marketers and content people who want to stop drafting by hand
  • Freelancers and consultants publishing regularly
  • Anyone posting to Instagram, LinkedIn, a blog, or a newsletter on a schedule
